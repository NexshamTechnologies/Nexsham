General
What is NexSham Technologies?−
NexSham Technologies is a forward-thinking technology company that delivers innovative digital solutions. We specialize in web development, mobile app development, cloud solutions, UI/UX design, DevOps, and more to help businesses grow and succeed.
Where is NexSham Technologies located?+
Our headquarters is located in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. We work with clients across the globe through remote collaboration and on-site engagements.
What industries do you serve?+
We work across fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, education, logistics, and real estate, adapting our solutions to each industry's specific needs.
How can I contact NexSham Technologies?+
You can reach us via phone, email, or the contact form on our website. Our team typically responds within 24 hours on business days.
Do you work with startups?+
Absolutely. We offer flexible engagement models designed for startups, including MVP development and scalable architecture planning.
What makes NexSham Technologies different?+
Our focus on transparency, dedicated teams, and long-term partnership sets us apart, along with our commitment to quality and on-time delivery.
Process
What is your development process?+
We follow an agile methodology with discovery, design, development, testing, and deployment phases, keeping you involved at every step.
How do you ensure project transparency?+
We provide regular status updates, shared project boards, and scheduled check-ins so you always know where your project stands.
How long does it take to complete a project?+
Timelines vary by scope, ranging from a few weeks for MVPs to several months for enterprise-grade platforms. We share estimates after discovery.
Will I be involved in the development process?+
Yes, we encourage close collaboration through regular demos and feedback sessions so the product matches your vision.
What happens after the project is completed?+
We offer post-launch support, monitoring, and maintenance packages to keep your product running smoothly.
Technologies
What technologies do you work with?+
We work with React.js, Next.js, Node.js, Python, AWS, and Docker, among other modern tools, chosen based on project needs.
Do you use open-source technologies?+
Yes, we leverage proven open-source frameworks and libraries whenever they add value, speed, and reliability to a project.
Can you work with our existing tech stack?+
Definitely. Our team can integrate with and extend your current systems rather than requiring a full rebuild.
Services
What services do you offer?+
We offer web development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, cloud solutions, DevOps & deployment, and software consulting.
Can you build custom software for my business?+
Yes, we specialize in building tailored software solutions designed around your specific workflows and business goals.
Do you offer fixed-price or hourly engagements?+
Both. We tailor the engagement model — fixed-price for well-defined scopes, or hourly/retainer for ongoing work.
Do you sign an NDA before starting a project?+
Yes, we're happy to sign an NDA to protect your idea and data before any project discussion begins.
Can you redesign an existing product?+
Yes, we offer redesign and modernization services for outdated products, improving both UX and underlying architecture.
Do you provide UI/UX design services separately?+
Yes, our design team offers standalone UI/UX design services, including wireframes, prototypes, and design systems.
Can you help with cloud migration?+
Yes, we help businesses migrate to AWS and other cloud platforms with minimal downtime and a clear migration plan.
Do you offer white-label development?+
Yes, we partner with agencies and businesses to deliver white-label development under their own brand.
Support
Do you provide support after deployment?+
Yes, we offer ongoing support and maintenance plans tailored to your product's needs after it goes live.
What kind of support do you offer?+
Our support covers bug fixes, performance monitoring, security updates, and feature enhancements as your business grows.
How can I raise a support request?+
You can raise a request through our support portal, email, or your dedicated account manager for faster resolution.
